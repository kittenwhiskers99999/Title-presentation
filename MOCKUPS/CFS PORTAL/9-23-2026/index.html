WELCOME TO CALABRIAN

A school small enough to know your child, serious enough to prepare them.

Calabrian Formation School, Inc. has served families in the community for over two decades, offering complete elementary education from Grade 1 through Grade 6. Our classrooms stay intentionally small so that no child slips through the cracks.

Rooted in Christian values and committed to academic excellence, we form learners who are curious, compassionate, and confident — prepared not only for high school, but for life.

WHO WE ARE

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide quality Catholic elementary education that nurtures the intellectual, spiritual, emotional, and social growth of every learner — forming children who think critically, act justly, and serve generously within their families and communities.

Our Vision

A learning community where every Calabrian child is known, valued, and formed into a Christ-centered individual — equipped with the knowledge, character, and compassion to become a force for good in the world.
